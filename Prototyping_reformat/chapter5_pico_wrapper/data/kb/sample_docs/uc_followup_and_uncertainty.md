# UC Follow-Up and Uncertainty Notes

## Follow-Up Horizon
Follow-up windows in study summaries are often short to medium term,
for example 8 to 12 weeks for early response and longer windows for maintenance.

## Evidence Limitations
- Heterogeneous inclusion criteria across studies can reduce direct comparability.
- Outcome definitions can vary across trials and cohorts.
- External validity can be limited for specific patient subgroups.

## Clinical Safety Language
Decision support outputs should state uncertainty and should request
clinician review when retrieved evidence is sparse or inconsistent.
