# UC Biologic Therapy Notes

## Population
Adults with moderate to severe ulcerative colitis who remain symptomatic
despite conventional treatment are frequently considered for biologic or targeted therapy classes.

## Comparator Framing
Common comparisons in evidence synthesis include:
- biologic class versus placebo,
- one advanced therapy class versus another class,
- escalation versus continuation of baseline management.

## Outcomes
Frequently reported outcomes include:
- clinical remission,
- endoscopic improvement,
- steroid-free remission,
- adverse events and discontinuation rates.
